    Where roofing leads leak

    You already pay for the leads. These are the five places they slip away.

    1. Missed calls

      On a roof, on the road, after hours.

    2. Slow response

      Web and ad leads that wait hours for a callback.

    3. Unqualified calls

      Out-of-area or wrong-job calls eating your time.

    4. Silent estimates

      Quotes sent, never followed up.

    5. Old leads

      Past inquiries sitting in your CRM, never contacted again.

    Jobstead plugs every one of these.

    What it does

    Your front desk, while you're on the roof.

    • Answers every call, 24/7

      In your company's name, nights, weekends and holidays included.

      "Thanks for calling Oak Ridge Roofing. How can I help?"

    • Qualifies every homeowner

      Address, leak or damage, roof age, insurance claim, repair or replacement, residential or commercial.

      "Is this a repair, or are you thinking about a full replacement?"

    • Checks your service area

      Only books jobs inside the radius and towns you choose.

      "We do cover Round Rock. Let's get you on the schedule."

    • Books inspections into your calendar

      Straight onto your schedule, with a text confirmation and reminders for the homeowner.

      "You're booked for Monday at 9 AM. We'll text you a reminder."

    • Texts back missed calls and web leads

      Anyone who hangs up, and every web lead, gets a reply within a minute.

      "Sorry we missed your call. How can we help with your roof?"

    • Follows up on unsold estimates

      Automatic check-ins on the quotes you've sent, so good jobs don't go cold.

      "Just checking in on the estimate we sent last week. Any questions?"

    • Reactivates old leads

      Reaches back out to past inquiries you never closed, from a list you share with us.

      "You asked us about an inspection in the spring. Still need a hand?"

    • Asks for Google reviews

      After the job, happy customers get a short review request with your link.

      "Thanks for choosing Oak Ridge Roofing. Would you leave us a quick review?"

    • Hands off to you when it matters

      Active leaks, angry callers and complex insurance questions go straight to you.

      "That sounds urgent. Let me get the owner on the line now."

    How much does it cost?

    $499 a month. Founding roofers pay no setup fee, and there's no contract. It's month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

    Do I keep my number?

    Yes. Your number stays the same. You forward missed and after-hours calls to a number we set up.

    What if a caller wants a real person?

    It transfers the call to you or takes a message, based on your rules. Active leaks, angry callers and complex insurance questions go straight to you.

    Storm Mode handles many calls at once, flags active leaks as urgent, captures the address, damage and insurance details, and books inspections in priority order. You see the full list and decide who to visit first.

    No. It checks your service radius and towns first, and only books jobs inside the area you choose.
