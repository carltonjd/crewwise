Last updated: September 30, 2026

Jobstead is provided by Hallmark Data Systems Pvt Ltd. We keep billing simple: one flat monthly price, no long contract, and you can cancel anytime.

Pricing

Jobstead costs $499 per month, billed monthly in advance in US dollars through Stripe. For founding roofers, the setup fee is waived. Plans are month-to-month.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel anytime. Email us at [EMAIL] from the address on your account, or call us at [US PHONE].
  • Your cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing month. Jobstead keeps answering your calls until then, and you will not be charged again.
  • To send calls straight back to your phone right away, turn off call forwarding at any time.
  • We will confirm your cancellation by email.

Refunds

  • Monthly fees are paid in advance and are not refunded for partly used months.
  • If you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or charged after your cancellation took effect, we will refund that charge in full.
  • To ask for a refund, email [EMAIL] with your business name and the date of the charge. We aim to reply within 2 business days.
  • Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show the refund.

Questions about a charge

If something on your bill looks wrong, please contact us first. We will look into it and put it right quickly.
