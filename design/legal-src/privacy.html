Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Jobstead is a brand of Hallmark Data Systems Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information we collect through our website and our service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, phone number and type of business when you fill in a form on our website.
  • Anything you tell us when you book or join a demo call, or when you email or call us.
  • Business and account details you give us when you sign up, such as your business name, hours, services, prices and how you want calls handled.
  • Billing information. Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number.

Information from calls and messages we handle for our clients

When Jobstead answers a call or exchanges text messages for one of our clients, we collect the person's phone number, a recording and transcript of the call or the messages, and details they share, such as their name, address, the job they need and the appointment they book. If a client asks us to follow up with past leads or customers, we use the contact details the client gives us. We process this information on behalf of our client.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it delivers them. We do not use advertising cookies.

How we use information

  • To reply to your inquiry and set up a demo.
  • To provide the service: answering calls, booking appointments, sending notifications, and producing recordings, transcripts and reports for our clients.
  • To bill clients and manage accounts.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and improve how it works.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Callers and our clients

If you called a business that uses Jobstead, that business decides how your information is used. If you want to access or delete information from your call, please contact that business. We will help them respond to your request.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with:

  • The client business the caller was trying to reach.
  • Service providers who help us run Jobstead, such as hosting, telephone, speech and AI processing, scheduling, form handling and payment processing (Stripe). They may use the information only to provide services to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is sold or merged.

We do not share mobile phone numbers or text message consent with third parties for their marketing.

How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as we need it to provide the service, meet our legal obligations and resolve disputes. Clients can ask us to delete call recordings and transcripts at any time.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where information is processed

We and our service providers may store and process information in countries other than the one you live in, including India and the United States.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, including some US states, you may have additional rights under local law. To make a request, email us at [EMAIL]. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Children

Jobstead is a service for businesses. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy
