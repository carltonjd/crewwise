# Home and site surface (Crewwise)

Scope: `/` plus every Phase 1 page sharing its world (pricing, free audit, roofing, legal, 404). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: professional firms (law, architecture, accounting) and home-service businesses; no clinics, on a phone, short on time, wary of hype. Job: understand in seconds that Crewwise gives them an AI receptionist that answers every call and books the work, from $299/month, plus websites and AI automation from the same team. Action: Get my free audit (`/free-audit/`). Proof: a labelled example of the first minute on a phone (Harbor & Lane Law, fictional), the demo call further down. Octalogic mentions are switched off until the partners agree. Constraints: fixed palette and Hanken Grotesk, only price "From $299/month", no invented stats, logos or testimonials.
Memorable moment: the first minute played on a cut-paper smartphone, ending in the booking slip.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Win the first minute, shown on the one object every new client uses: their phone. A Saul Bass style cut-paper world on a full-bleed Crewwise blue field, where the firm's website, the tap on Call now, the call answered by the AI front desk and the booking all happen on one flat cut-paper smartphone. It refuses timers, dashboards, card grids and stock imagery.

OWN-WORLD: Flat Crewwise blue #1E5BD8 field, white type, navy #0F1B33 cut-paper shapes with slightly irregular hand-cut edges, depth from flat overlapping planes of blue / blue-deep #1749B0 / navy (NO hard zero-blur offset shadows: owner rejected them as an AI tell, 2026-11-03), white slips get only the soft --shadow-paper; white cut-paper slips for results (green check only on a real success state); orange only on the action; Hanken Grotesk 800-900 headlines, tight tracking, never tighter than -0.04em.

STORY: The visitor reads the promise, watches a new client find Harbor & Lane Law's site on a phone, tap Call now, get answered by the AI front desk and get booked, then acts on the free audit. Below, the page carries the same cut-paper language through the demo, services and close.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blue header (white logo and nav, orange button) on the blue field. H4, stacked stage, as built: the headline runs two lines across the top in white; under it the sub-line, orange Get my free audit, white Hear it answer a call link and the note. One big flat 2D cut-paper smartphone stands right of centre, starting beside the headline and cropped by the fold; no tilt in 3D, no parallax. A blue-deep paper floor with an irregular hand-cut pale edge runs the full width just under the call to action, so the copy and the phone stand on the same ground (the fine print sits on it). A darker --blue-sheet offset sheet sits behind the phone. The story plays when the artwork scrolls into view: website, abstract tap ripple on Call now, Calling, Answered by the AI front desk, call ended with "Booked Thu 10:00 AM", then the white "Consultation booked, Thu 10:00 AM" slip slides in at the phone's lower left and bridges toward the copy. A small Replay link appears once it has played. Phone: copy first with the button above the fold, the phone below with Call now still in the first screen.

FORM: Surface composition G2a "Tap to call", chosen by the owner on 2026-11-02, built as the stacked H4 variant (owner, 2026-11-03). Signature interaction: the whole first minute plays on the phone's screen and ends in the booking slip. Motion: one sequence that starts when the artwork scrolls into view, replayable; transform and opacity only; no tilt or parallax; the phone and all layers stay flat 2D so text is crisp; static final state under reduced motion and without JS. Planes (floor, sheet) have slightly irregular hand-cut edges.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
