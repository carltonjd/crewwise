## How to cancel

- Email [hello@crewwisehq.com](mailto:hello@crewwisehq.com) from the address on your account.
- Your cancellation takes effect 30 days after we receive your notice. Your services continue until then.
- To send calls straight back to your own phone right away, turn off call forwarding at any time.
- Website plan: when the plan ends, we take the website offline. You keep your domain, your logo, your photos and the text you gave us. Section 5 of our [client terms](/terms/) explains what happens to your website.
- We confirm every cancellation by email.

## Refunds

- Monthly fees are paid in advance. Fees already paid are not refunded, except where the law requires it.
- If you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or charged after your cancellation took effect, we refund that charge in full.
- To ask for a refund, email us with your business name and the date of the charge. We aim to reply within two business days.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show them.
